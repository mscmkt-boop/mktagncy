What Is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)? A Practical Definition

By Legwork Editorial Team · January 12, 2026

  • AEO
  • GEO
  • AI search

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring content so AI systems — Google’s AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — can accurately extract it and cite it when generating a direct answer to a user’s question.

Why this is different from traditional SEO

Traditional SEO optimizes for ranking position: where a page lands on a results page a human will scroll through. AEO optimizes for extraction: whether a system can pull a clean, accurate answer out of your content without a human ever visiting the page at all.

That means the same page can rank well and still get zero AI citations, if the actual answer is buried in the fourth paragraph instead of stated clearly up front.

The core mechanics

A few patterns show up consistently in content that gets cited by answer engines:

  • A direct, self-contained answer within the first 1-2 sentences of a section
  • Consistent naming for the same entity or concept throughout the page
  • Explicit question-and-answer formatting, ideally backed by FAQPage schema
  • A strong technical and authority foundation, since answer engines still weigh trust signals before citing a source

Where this fits with the rest of an SEO program

AEO isn’t a replacement for technical SEO, content strategy, or link building — it’s a layer on top of them. Sites with weak crawlability or thin authority don’t tend to earn AI citations regardless of how well individual pages are formatted. Read more in our AEO/GEO service overview.

Frequently asked questions

Is AEO the same thing as GEO?+

They're used near-interchangeably. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) emphasizes structuring content to be extracted as a direct answer; GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a broader term covering visibility across generative AI tools generally. In practice, the tactics overlap almost entirely.
