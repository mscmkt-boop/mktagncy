Off-page SEO covers the signals that happen outside your own site — primarily backlinks, but also brand mentions, citations, and third-party signals that tell search engines your site is a credible, authoritative source.

## Why authority still decides close rankings

When two pages are otherwise well-optimized, backlink authority is frequently the deciding factor in who ranks higher. That hasn't changed with the rise of AI search — answer engines still lean on established authority signals when deciding which sources to trust and cite.

## How we build links

We prioritize earned links over purchased ones: digital PR campaigns built around original research or data, editorial outreach tied to content that's genuinely useful to link to, and reclaiming unlinked brand mentions that already exist. Every campaign is measured against a target list of relevant, authoritative domains rather than raw link volume, because ten links from sites your buyers actually read outperform a hundred from link farms.
