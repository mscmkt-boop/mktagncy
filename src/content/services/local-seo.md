Local SEO is the practice of optimizing a business's online presence to rank in map pack results, "near me" searches, and localized organic results — the searches that most directly precede a store visit, call, or booking.

## The map pack is a different ranking system

Local pack rankings weigh proximity, Google Business Profile completeness and activity, review volume and recency, and citation consistency alongside traditional on-page and authority signals. A site can rank well organically and still lose the map pack to a competitor with a more active, better-optimized profile.

## Multi-location complexity

For businesses with more than one location, the hard part is structure: each location needs a genuinely useful, differentiated landing page and its own set of local signals, without triggering duplicate content problems from templated pages. We build location page templates with real local specificity — staff, service-area nuance, localized reviews and schema — rather than the same paragraph with the city name swapped.
