An SEO audit is a comprehensive diagnostic of a site's current search and AI visibility performance across technical health, on-page quality, content depth, and backlink authority, ending in a prioritized, actionable roadmap.

## What separates a useful audit from a template report

Automated tools can generate a 40-page audit from a crawl in minutes, but most of what's in those reports is generic and unprioritized. Our audits start from the same crawl and log file data but add manual review — what's actually causing the biggest gap between your site and the pages outranking you, not just a list of every technical imperfection regardless of impact.

## What's included

Every audit covers technical crawlability, on-page and content quality relative to top-ranking competitors, backlink authority gaps, and — as a standard baseline now — how visible the brand currently is across AI Overviews and conversational search tools for priority queries. The deliverable is a roadmap ranked by projected impact against implementation effort, so you know what to fix first regardless of who does the fixing.
