Analytics and reporting is the measurement layer underneath every other service: GA4 for behavior and conversions, Google Search Console for how Google sees and ranks the site, and Google Tag Manager for the tracking infrastructure connecting the two.

## Bad measurement makes every other decision worse

We regularly find GA4 properties with broken conversion events, GTM containers with duplicate or conflicting tags, or Search Console properties that aren't verified for all relevant domains and subdomains. Any of these quietly invalidates the data used to make content, technical, and budget decisions — so we treat a measurement audit as a prerequisite, not an add-on.

## Reporting built around outcomes

Traffic and ranking reports are only useful as leading indicators. Our standard reporting ties SEO activity to the metrics that actually matter to a marketing director or founder: organic-driven leads or revenue, conversion rate trends by landing page type, and — where relevant — brand citation frequency across AI answer engines.
