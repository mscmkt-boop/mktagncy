Content strategy is the planning layer that decides what to publish, why, and in what order, so that content builds topical authority instead of accumulating as a pile of disconnected blog posts.

## Topical authority beats one-off posts

Search engines and AI answer engines increasingly reward sites that demonstrate comprehensive, connected coverage of a subject area over sites with a few high-effort posts. That means content strategy is really an information architecture problem: mapping the full set of questions and subtopics your audience needs answered, then sequencing production so pillar and supporting content reinforce each other through internal linking.

## Where content marketing meets AEO

The same structural qualities that make content easy for readers to scan — clear headers, direct answers up front, well-organized FAQ sections — are what make it easy for AI Overviews and answer engines to extract and cite. We build that structure into every brief by default rather than retrofitting it later, which is the difference between content that ranks and content that also gets cited by AI search.
