AEO and GEO are the practice of optimizing content so AI answer engines — Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and similar tools — can accurately parse, extract, and cite it when generating a direct answer to a user's question.

## Why this is a layer, not a replacement

Nothing about AEO invalidates traditional SEO. Answer engines still rely heavily on crawlability, established authority, and structured data to decide what's trustworthy enough to cite — all core SEO fundamentals. What's different is the content shape that gets pulled into a generated answer: concise, self-contained, well-labeled answers near the top of a page, consistent entity naming, and explicit FAQ structure outperform content that requires the reader (or the model) to piece an answer together from context.

## What we actually optimize

We audit how your brand currently shows up — or doesn't — across AI Overviews and the major conversational search tools for a defined set of priority queries. From there, the work is concrete: restructuring key pages so a direct, quotable answer appears before the supporting depth, building out FAQ blocks with explicit FAQPage schema, tightening entity naming so your brand and offerings are described consistently across the site, and strengthening the technical and authority foundation that makes AI systems more willing to cite a source at all.

## How this fits with the rest of your SEO program

AEO work compounds with [technical SEO](/services/technical-seo), [schema markup](/services/schema-structured-data), and [content strategy](/services/content-strategy-marketing) — it is not a separate content track. Every new piece of content we plan gets structured with both traditional ranking factors and AI-extraction in mind from the brief stage forward.
