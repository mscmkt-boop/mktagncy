On-page SEO is the optimization of individual page elements — titles, headings, body content, internal links, and markup — so that both search engines and AI answer engines can quickly determine what a page is about and whether it answers the query well.

## What good on-page work looks like now

The mechanics haven't changed much: relevant title tags, one clear H1, a logical heading hierarchy with no skipped levels, and content that actually matches search intent instead of padding a target keyword count. What has changed is the payoff. With AI Overviews and answer engines increasingly extracting short, self-contained answers, pages that lead with a direct, quotable answer before going into depth outperform pages that build up to the point.

We map every page to a specific intent and query cluster, then structure the content so the direct answer sits at the top, followed by the supporting depth that keeps human readers — and dwell time — on the page.

## Internal linking as a signal, not an afterthought

Internal links tell search engines which pages you consider most important and how topics relate to each other. We build linking structures around topic clusters (a pillar page supported by related sub-pages) rather than ad hoc links dropped in wherever they fit editorially.
