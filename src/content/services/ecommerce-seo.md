E-commerce SEO applies technical, on-page, and structured data work to the specific challenges of product catalogs: scale, faceted navigation, frequent inventory changes, and product schema that needs to stay accurate in near real time.

## Scale changes the problem

A 50-page marketing site and a 50,000-SKU catalog are not the same optimization problem. At catalog scale, template-level decisions — how category pages handle pagination, how filters generate or don't generate indexable URLs, how out-of-stock products are handled — have outsized impact because they're multiplied across thousands of pages at once.

## Product schema and shopping answer engines

Accurate Product, Offer, and Review schema isn't optional at this point — it feeds Google's shopping features, rich results, and increasingly the product recommendations surfaced directly inside AI answer engines. We align product schema with your merchant feed so pricing, availability, and review data stay consistent across every surface that reads it.
