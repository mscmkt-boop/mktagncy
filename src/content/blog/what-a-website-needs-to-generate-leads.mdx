A website can look polished and still generate almost no leads, because looking good and being built to convert are two different jobs. Most of what actually drives leads is structural, not visual.

## What actually moves the needle

- **A clear, repeated call to action.** Not buried in a footer — visible on every page, saying exactly what you want a visitor to do next (call, book, request a quote).
- **Speed.** A visitor decides whether to stay within a couple of seconds. A slow site loses people before they ever see the offer.
- **A short path from landing to contact.** Every extra click or form field between "interested" and "submitted" costs conversions.
- **Basic SEO from day one.** A beautiful site nobody can find through search isn't generating leads either — it's generating a hosting bill.
- **Mobile-first design.** For most small businesses, more than half of traffic is on a phone; a desktop-first design that's merely "responsive" often loses those visitors on usability, not looks.

## Why this gets skipped

These aren't expensive to build — they're just easy to deprioritize in favor of things that are more fun to design, like a custom animation or an elaborate homepage layout. The fix isn't a bigger budget, it's building the conversion path first and the visual polish around it, not the other way around.
