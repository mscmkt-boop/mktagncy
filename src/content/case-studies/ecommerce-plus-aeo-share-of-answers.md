## The full story

The brand ranked well in traditional organic results but was invisible in AI Overviews for the exact category and comparison queries where competitors were being cited directly. The root cause was two-layered: inconsistent schema implementation meant search engines couldn't confidently parse product data at scale, and the buying-guide content itself buried useful answers inside long narrative paragraphs instead of leading with them.

We standardized Product, Offer, and AggregateRating schema across the full catalog and validated every template against Google's Rich Results Test, which alone fixed a meaningful share of rich-result eligibility gaps. On the content side, buying guides were restructured so each subsection opened with a direct, one-to-two sentence answer to the implied question, followed by supporting depth — the same structure AI Overviews were already extracting from competitor pages.

FAQ sections were added to every core category and guide page, built from the actual questions showing up in existing AI Overview answers and customer support tickets, each marked up with explicit FAQPage schema.
