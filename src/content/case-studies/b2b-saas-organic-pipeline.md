## The full story

The blog had volume — over 200 published posts — but no structure. Topics were chosen reactively, cannibalized each other for the same keywords, and almost none were built with a clear next step for a reader who was ready to evaluate the product.

We started with consolidation: more than 60 thin or overlapping posts were merged into stronger pillar pages or redirected outright, recovering link equity that had been split across near-duplicate content. From there, we mapped a topical architecture tied directly to the buyer journey — awareness-stage cluster content linking into comparison and use-case pages designed to convert.

Every new brief specified not just the target query but the intended next action for the reader, and GA4 was reconfigured to track that path end to end so demand gen and sales could see organic's actual contribution instead of relying on last-click, top-of-funnel traffic numbers.
