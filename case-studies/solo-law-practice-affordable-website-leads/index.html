Website Design & Development Case Study: Independent family law attorney

The challenge

No real website — just a Facebook page — made it hard to look credible against larger firms, and two prior agency quotes came in at $12k-$18k with 3-4 month timelines she couldn't justify.

The approach

Built and launched a focused, mobile-first site in three weeks: clear consultation-booking CTA on every page, on-page SEO and schema from day one, and a Google Business Profile and local SEO setup to build credibility and local visibility together.

The full story

Referrals had carried the practice for years, but every prospective client’s first move was to search the attorney’s name, and a Facebook page with a handful of posts didn’t inspire confidence against firms with polished sites. Two prior agency quotes — $12k and $18k, both with 3-4 month timelines — made a real website feel out of reach for a solo practice.

The build focused on what actually mattered for a professional services site: a clear, repeated “Schedule a consultation” call to action, practice area pages structured around the actual questions prospective clients search for, and basic on-page SEO and schema in place at launch rather than added later. A Google Business Profile setup and local citation cleanup ran in parallel, since local credibility and local search visibility are effectively the same problem for a single-location practice.

The site launched in three weeks. Consultation requests were up 140% within the first 90 days, measured against the same quarter the year before — not from a traffic explosion, but from a site that finally gave people a reason to act instead of just a phone number to write down.

“I'd been quoted five figures and months of waiting just to get a website. This was done in three weeks and people finally take me seriously when they land on it — and they actually book.”

Founding Attorney, Independent family law practice
