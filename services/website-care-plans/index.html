Website Care Plans

Ongoing updates, uptime monitoring, and small content edits handled for you — so a website is a launch, not a second job.

A website that launches well and then gets ignored degrades quietly: plugins go out of date, a broken link creeps in, a form stops submitting and nobody notices until a lead complains. Website Care Plans keep a site healthy after launch, so staying on top of it isn’t one more thing on your plate.

The point is taking it off your list

You didn’t get into your business to also become your own webmaster. A care plan means updates, monitoring, and small content changes happen on a schedule in the background, and the only time you hear from us is when something actually needs your input — not every time a plugin needs a version bump.

What this isn’t

This isn’t a retainer that quietly does nothing until you cancel it. Every plan includes a real monthly check-in on uptime, performance, and Core Web Vitals, and a bank of included edits you can actually use — not a vague promise of “support” with no defined scope.

Key deliverables

  • Software, plugin, and security updates on a regular schedule
  • Uptime and broken-link monitoring with alerts
  • A monthly bank of small content edits (copy, images, hours, pricing) with no separate invoice
  • Automated backups with tested restore points
  • Monthly performance and Core Web Vitals check-in

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a 'small edit'?+

Swapping copy or images, updating hours or pricing, adding a team member or testimonial, fixing a typo — the kind of change that shouldn't require you to learn a CMS or wait on an invoice. Larger changes (new pages, redesigns, new features) are scoped separately.

What happens if I don't have a care plan and something breaks?+

We can still fix it, billed as a one-off. A care plan exists because most breakage — an expired plugin, a lapsed SSL cert, a hosting issue — is cheaper to prevent on a schedule than to fix in an emergency, and it means you're not the one who has to notice it first.
