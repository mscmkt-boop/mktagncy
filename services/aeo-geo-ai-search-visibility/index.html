SEO & AI Search Visibility

AEO/GEO (AI Search Visibility)

Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization to earn citations inside AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

AEO and GEO are the practice of optimizing content so AI answer engines — Google’s AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and similar tools — can accurately parse, extract, and cite it when generating a direct answer to a user’s question.

Why this is a layer, not a replacement

Nothing about AEO invalidates traditional SEO. Answer engines still rely heavily on crawlability, established authority, and structured data to decide what’s trustworthy enough to cite — all core SEO fundamentals. What’s different is the content shape that gets pulled into a generated answer: concise, self-contained, well-labeled answers near the top of a page, consistent entity naming, and explicit FAQ structure outperform content that requires the reader (or the model) to piece an answer together from context.

What we actually optimize

We audit how your brand currently shows up — or doesn’t — across AI Overviews and the major conversational search tools for a defined set of priority queries. From there, the work is concrete: restructuring key pages so a direct, quotable answer appears before the supporting depth, building out FAQ blocks with explicit FAQPage schema, tightening entity naming so your brand and offerings are described consistently across the site, and strengthening the technical and authority foundation that makes AI systems more willing to cite a source at all.

How this fits with the rest of your SEO program

AEO work compounds with technical SEO, schema markup, and content strategy — it is not a separate content track. Every new piece of content we plan gets structured with both traditional ranking factors and AI-extraction in mind from the brief stage forward.

Frequently asked questions

What is AEO/GEO, exactly?+

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) are the practice of structuring content so AI systems — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — can accurately extract and cite it as a source in generated answers. It's the AI-search counterpart to traditional SEO's focus on ranking positions.

Is AEO replacing traditional SEO?+

No. Traditional organic search still drives the majority of most clients' traffic, and the fundamentals overlap heavily: crawlability, entity clarity, and well-structured content help both. AEO is an added layer on top of solid SEO, not a replacement for it.

How do you measure AEO results if there's no ranking position?+

We track share-of-answer: how often your brand or content is cited across a defined set of AI search tools for your priority queries, alongside referral traffic from AI platforms where it's measurable and brand mention monitoring across generative answers.
