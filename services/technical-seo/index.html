Technical SEO

Crawlability, indexation, and site architecture work that removes the ceiling on everything else you publish.

Technical SEO is the discipline of making sure search engines and AI crawlers can find, render, and understand every page you want ranked. If Googlebot can’t crawl it efficiently or a page renders differently for a bot than a browser, no amount of content or backlinks will fix it.

What we actually do

Most technical SEO problems live below what a standard audit tool flags. We start with log file analysis to see how Googlebot is actually spending crawl budget on your site, not just what a simulated crawler thinks should happen. That’s paired with a full architectural review: how pages link to each other, whether your important pages are within a reasonable click depth of the homepage, and where duplicate or near-duplicate content is splitting ranking signals across URLs that should be one.

For JavaScript-heavy and headless sites, we specifically test rendered vs. served HTML, because a page that looks fine in a browser can be functionally invisible to a crawler if content only appears after client-side hydration.

Where this fits with everything else

Technical SEO is foundational work — it’s what makes on-page, content, and AEO/GEO work actually pay off. We typically run a technical audit first in any new engagement, because it tells us whether the site can support the growth targets before we invest in anything built on top of it.

Key deliverables

  • Full crawl audit (log files + Screaming Frog/Sitebulb) with prioritized fix list
  • Indexation and crawl budget analysis for sites over 10k URLs
  • Site architecture and internal linking restructure
  • Canonicalization, pagination, and faceted navigation cleanup
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt configuration
  • JavaScript rendering and hydration audits for SPA/headless stacks

Frequently asked questions

How is technical SEO different from a general site audit?+

A general audit surfaces symptoms. Technical SEO work traces those symptoms back to crawl, rendering, or architecture root causes and fixes them at that level, so the same problem doesn't resurface after the next content push or redesign.

How long does a technical SEO engagement usually take?+

Discovery and audit typically run 2-3 weeks depending on site size. Implementation timelines depend on your dev resourcing, but most clients see indexation and crawl efficiency improvements within 60-90 days of fixes shipping.
