Web Development

Website Design & Development

A fast, mobile-first website built and launched without the agency markup or the months-long timeline.

A website is a revenue channel and a credibility signal, not a line-item expense to minimize. Website Design & Development is the core build service: a fast, mobile-first site that actually represents your business and gives people a reason to book, call, or buy — done without the six-figure agency overhead or the six-month timeline.

Built to be found, not just built to look good

Every site we build starts with the same technical foundation as our SEO work: clean semantic HTML, fast load times, and basic schema markup from day one. That means you’re not paying separately later to “fix the SEO” on a site that was never built with it in mind — visibility is part of the build, not an upsell after the fact.

What “affordable” actually means here

Affordable doesn’t mean template-and-hope. It means cutting the overhead that doesn’t affect your outcome — no bloated CMS plugins, no bespoke backend you don’t need, no months of internal agency process — while keeping the parts that do affect your outcome: real design attention, real performance, and a build that’s actually yours when it’s done.

Where this fits with everything else

A new site with no visibility plan behind it is a business card nobody sees. We pair every build with SEO fundamentals from day one and can layer in ongoing care and updates or a full SEO/GEO program once the site is live — so the build is the start of a growth plan, not the end of the project.

Key deliverables

  • Custom design built around your services and how customers actually book/buy
  • Mobile-first, responsive build tested across real devices
  • Fast by default — same performance discipline as our SEO work (see Core Web Vitals)
  • Copywriting support so the site doesn't launch with placeholder text
  • Basic on-page SEO and schema baked in at launch, not bolted on later
  • Launch checklist: analytics, forms, SSL, redirects from any old site

Frequently asked questions

Why are website builds usually so expensive?+

A lot of agency pricing bakes in overhead — account managers, custom CMS builds, redesign cycles — that a straightforward business site doesn't need. We build on a proven, fast Astro-based foundation and reuse a component system across projects, which is what keeps the price down without cutting corners on speed or design quality.

How long does a typical build take?+

Most standalone business sites launch in 3-4 weeks from kickoff, assuming reasonably quick turnaround on your content and feedback. Larger or e-commerce builds take longer — we'll give you a real timeline before you commit, not an optimistic one.

Do I own the site after it's built?+

Yes. You get the code and content in a standard, portable format — no proprietary page builder locking you into us for hosting or edits.
